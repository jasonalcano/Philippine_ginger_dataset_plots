Philippine Ginger Metabolomics Visualization

This data visualization maps the current chemical diversity of metabolites across different plant tissues of two ginger species

Metabolite Relationships (Chord Diagram)

Chord diagram visualizes the unique occurence and co-occurrence of metabolite classes found across rhizome, leaves, inflorescence and infrusctence parts

Plant Part → Chemical Class → Metabolite Flow Distribution (Sankey Diagram)

Sankey diagram traces the distribution of metabolites from specific plant tissues (rhizomes, leaves, stems) through their respective chemical classifications.

heatmap showing presence and absence of metabolites across different plant tissues
